II

The Pancreatic Tumor Microenvironment (PDAC TME)

Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) remains a lethal malignancy with a 5-year survival rate below 12%. A key challenge is the dense desmoplastic stroma that acts as a physical and immunological barrier.

Desmoplastic Stroma: Abundant cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs) and extracellular matrix elements.
Immunological Barrier: Immunosuppressive stroma limits T-cell infiltration and efficacy.
Biomarker Scarcity: Lack of single tumor-specific antigens, causing off-target safety risks.

TME Structural Layers

Dense Fibrotic Stroma (CAFs & Collagen)
Immunosuppressive Cells (MDSCs, Tregs)
Malignant Epithelial Cells (PDAC)
III

Why a Single Marker is Insufficient

Standard targeting strategies rely on highly overexpressed single proteins (e.g., MSLN or CEA). However, these markers are frequently expressed at lower levels in healthy tissues (e.g., gastrointestinal tract, lung).

On-Target, Off-Tumor Toxicity

Single-antigen chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) therapies cannot distinguish between low-expressing healthy tissue and high-expressing tumor tissue, causing severe systemic toxicity.

Toxicity Scenario

Healthy Cell Low Expression
Single CAR-T Cell
Toxicity Triggered (OFF-Target)
IV

The AND-Gate Biosensor Paradigm

To prevent off-target toxicity, we engineer logic-gated synthetic circuits that require the simultaneous presence of two distinct inputs (Input A AND Input B) to trigger activation.

Animation concept: Watch how single-marker overlaps resolve into a strict 2D decision boundary.
V

Discovery Cohort & Sample Size

To build a robust and unbiased pipeline, we combined sequencing data from primary pancreatic tumors (TCGA-PAAD) and healthy pancreas tissue (GTEx). Values were harmonized using the TOIL pipeline.

Microarray Validation: For external validation, the GSE62452 dataset from GEO was used to test cross-platform transferability.

VI

Unbiased Selection Pipeline

Our pipeline proceeds through nine steps to identify candidate pairs starting from genome-wide transcripts down to math modeling and validation.

VII

First-Pass Screening (Welch's t-test)

Differential expression (DE) comparing 178 tumors against 167 normal tissues identified 19,399 tumor-high candidate genes (log2FC ≥ 1.0, FDR < 0.05).

Specificity Scoring: Prioritized genes using the product of single-gene AUC and log2 fold change.
Top Candidates: Highlighted UBE2S and CCR6 as significant upregulated candidate genes.
VIII

ML Prioritization & SHAP Attribution

An L1-regularized Logistic Regression model achieved perfect cross-validation AUC (1.000). Features were prioritized using SHAP value attributions.

Model Attribution vs. Causality

SHAP values reflect model feature importance, explaining how predictions are built. They do not directly prove biological causality.

IX

Selected Input Candidate: UBE2S + CCR6

We evaluated all combinations of the top 100 SHAP genes using a composite Pair Score based on pairwise correlation and tumor activation rates.

UBE2S (Input A): Proliferation / cell-cycle axis.
CCR6 (Input B): Immune stroma / chemokine receptor axis.
Pearson correlation: r = 0.714. Functionally divergent pathways, but statistically correlated in bulk tissue.
X

Mathematical Hill-Equation Modeling

The AND gate is simulated using a dual-input Hill equation modeling continuous logic circuit activation kinetics.

$$Output = P_{\text{basal}} + V_{\max} \cdot H(A) \cdot H(B)$$
$$H(X) = \frac{[X]^n}{K_X^n + [X]^n}$$
Model parameters K_A (0.760) and K_B (0.464) are transcriptomic thresholds, not physical dissociation constants.
XI

Validation and Transferability

The optimized AND gate was validated on the independent GSE62452 dataset. While specificity remains exceptionally high, sensitivity drops significantly due to platform differences (microarray vs. RNA-seq).

"high-specificity candidate" ≠ "validated biosensor"
XII

Summary & Wet-Lab Translation

Our unbiased computational pipeline prioritized candidate pairs for a logic-gated synthetic biosensor. Wet-lab validation is the next critical step.

Synthetic Promoters: Drive orthogonal transcription factors in split-transactivator configurations.
RNA-Based Sensors: Leverage toehold switches for direct intracellular transcript sensing.
In Vitro Validation: Test circuit activation in target PDAC cell lines (PANC-1) vs. healthy epithelia (HPDE).
