01 · Clinical Context

The Target Challenge of Pancreatic Adenocarcinoma

PDAC is notoriously resistant to conventional therapies, largely due to its complex and protective microenvironment.

  • Desmoplastic Stroma: A dense extracellular matrix acts as a physical barrier to drug delivery.
  • Immunosuppressive Niche: Stromal signals exclude active cytotoxic T-cells and recruit suppressor cells.
  • No Unique Tumor Markers: Single target antigens (e.g. MSLN, CEA) are shared with healthy tissues.
  • High Off-Target Risk: Single-input therapeutics face severe on-target, off-tumor toxicities.

02 · Design Principles

AND-Gate Logic Enhances Targeting Specificity

By requiring two independent markers to be present simultaneously, we restrict biosensor activation only to the core tumor area.

  • Boolean Logic: The circuit produces a therapeutic or diagnostic output (ON) only when Input A AND Input B are present.
  • Molecular Choice: Input A (UBE2S): High in rapidly dividing cells (malignant proliferation). Input B (CCR6): High in inflammatory recruitment areas (stroma/immune niche).
  • Safety Margin: Avoids triggering in normal pancreas (low inputs) or inflammatory-only/proliferation-only normal organs.

High (Proliferating Only) Low OFF (No Activation)

High (Malignant) High (Stromal Recruitment) ON (Active Output)

05 · Transcriptomics

First-Pass Sieve: Differential Expression Analysis

Figure 1 · Volcano Plot of Discovery Cohort. High statistical significance ($-\log_{10} P$) and fold changes ($\log_2 \text{FC}$) separate tumor from normal tissues.

Differential expression establishes statistical significance, but statistical rankings do not directly yield safe biosensor candidates.

  • High Statistical Power: Thousands of genes pass strict FDR thresholds, reflecting massive tissue dysregulation.
  • Abundant Marker Bias: Top-ranked DE genes often represent highly abundant structural or metabolic genes (e.g. keratins, ribosomal genes).
  • Limitation: High fold-change does not mean zero expression in healthy organs. We require multi-dimensional screening to filter out off-target risks.

06 · Machine Learning

Feature Prioritization and SHAP Interpretation

Figure 2 · SHAP Feature Importance. XGBoost feature contributions demonstrate the ranking of predictive biomarkers.

Machine learning was used as a biomarker discovery tool to isolate feature dependencies, rather than as a clinical diagnostics classifier.

  • Algorithms: L1 Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost were tested.
  • SHAP Interpretation: SHAP value distributions determine which features drive the model's prediction of malignancy.
  • Threshold Extraction: SHAP dependence inflection points indicate where model prediction shifts, establishing the theoretical biosensor activation bounds.
  • Batch Caveat: Excellent discovery performance (AUC ~ 1.0) is partially driven by cohort batch differences between TCGA and GTEx.

07 · Marker Selection

Complementary Axes: UBE2S and CCR6

The chosen logic pair represents two distinct, complementary biological signatures of the pancreatic tumor microenvironment.

  • UBE2S (Tumor Proliferation Axis): Ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme E2 S. Strongly upregulated during cell cycle progression. Captures the rapid mitosis hallmark of malignant cells.
  • CCR6 (Immune Microenvironment Axis): C-C Motif Chemokine Receptor 6. Active in lymphatic and dendritic cell recruitment. Captures stromal inflammatory signaling and immune infiltration.
  • Complementarity: Combining a cell-cycle marker with an immunological marker reduces off-target triggers in proliferating non-tumor cells or inflamed healthy organs.

09 · Mathematical Modeling

Phenomenological Logic Gate Formulation

Figure 4 · Simulated AND-Gate Output. Simulated response surface as a function of UBE2S and CCR6 expression inputs.

We simulated logical behavior using a phenomenological Hill-equation model to verify threshold sensitivity.

Output = P_{basal} + V_{max} \times H(\text{UBE2S}) \times H(\text{CCR6})

  • Hill Cooperativity: Hill coefficient ($n = 2$) controls switch sharpness.
  • Phenomenological Coefficients: Activation thresholds ($K_A$ and $K_B$) represent mathematical parameters inferred from the SHAP model, not biochemical dissociation constants.
  • Significance: The model evaluates the mathematical feasibility of the gate logic, laying a template for downstream synthetic gene circuit construction.

11 · Discussion

Limitations & Next Steps

This study represents an in silico design hypothesis. Translating it to clinical utility requires addressing several limitations.

  • Platform Batch Effects: Standardizing thresholds across different sequencing platforms and assays.
  • Spatial Verification: Transitioning from bulk tissue to spatial transcriptomics to verify UBE2S and CCR6 co-localization.
  • Wet-Lab Verification: Clone circuit into lentivirus. Evaluate activation in human PDAC cell lines (e.g. PANC-1) and non-tumor control cells via qPCR and GFP reporters.

Take-Home Message

"This pipeline establishes a structured framework to convert raw public transcriptomic data into testable, high-specificity synthetic biology design hypotheses."
